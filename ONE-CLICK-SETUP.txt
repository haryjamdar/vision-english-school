THE VISION ENGLISH SCHOOL - ONE CLICK SETUP

1. Install Node.js LTS once:
   https://nodejs.org/

2. Extract this ZIP anywhere on your Windows PC.

3. Double-click:
   START WEBSITE.bat

On the first run, it automatically runs npm install.
After that it starts:
- Website: http://127.0.0.1:5173
- Local data server: http://127.0.0.1:8787

Your local data is stored in:
server\data\site-data.json

To stop the website, close the two command windows or double-click:
STOP WEBSITE.bat
